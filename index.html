THE PROBLEM

Heat makes hard work harder.

High-temperature environments can make physical work uncomfortable and reduce productivity. Just Chill explores an active, wearable approach to cooling.

40°C+Extreme heat example
OUR SOLUTION

Active cooling, built into the jacket.

The system combines thermoelectric cooling, heat dissipation, airflow and monitoring in one wearable platform.

HOW IT WORKS

Peltier cooling, simplified.

Electricity drives heat transfer from the cold side to the hot side. The hot side then needs effective heat removal.
